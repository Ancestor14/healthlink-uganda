First Aid Tips

Bleeding Control

Apply direct pressure with a clean cloth. Elevate the wound above heart level if possible. Continue pressure for at least 10 minutes.

Burns

Cool the burn with running cool water for 10-15 minutes. Do not use ice. Cover with a clean, dry cloth. Do not apply butter or ointments.

CPR Instructions

Push hard and fast in the center of the chest at 100-120 compressions per minute. Use an AED if available. Continue until help arrives.

Allergic Reactions

Use an epinephrine auto-injector if available. Administer antihistamines. Keep the person calm and monitor breathing.

Fractures

Immobilize the injured area. Apply ice to reduce swelling. Do not try to realign bones. Seek immediate medical attention.

Concussion

Keep the person awake and alert. Monitor for confusion or dizziness. Apply ice to swollen areas. Seek medical evaluation.
